HX Effects · NAM, Amps and Effects

Put NAM captures and amps on your HX Effects

This page does everything for you, step by step: it makes the new firmware from Line 6's own file, shows you how to install it, and turns your .nam captures into files the HX Effects can play. It works in your browser on a Mac or a Windows PC. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

▶ Video tutorial: how to set it up and run it, step by step (opens in a new tab).

Firmware installed? Build presets for it, with NAM, the amps, the cabs and the new effects, in the preset builder: it sends them straight to the unit.

Make your own drum grooves for Drum Chapel, or start from rock beats, in the groove editor: a step grid that previews every kit and sends the grooves to the unit.

Unofficial and experimental. This isn't made or supported by Line 6. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk and may void your warranty; it's your responsibility, and no liability is accepted (disclaimer at the bottom). If anything goes wrong, Rescue (at the bottom) puts the normal firmware back, from this page or with Line 6 Updater; your presets and IRs survive it.
Good to know before you start
  • HX Edit only works for loading files. You can still use it to import IRs and NAM captures (the .wav files from step 4) and to back up. It can't show or edit NAM, the amps, the cabs or the new effects: build and edit presets that use them on the HX Effects itself, or in the preset builder.
  • Too many heavy blocks in one preset can freeze or restart the unit. NAM, the amps and some of the effects use a lot of the processor. Add heavy blocks one at a time, and save your presets often so nothing is lost if the unit restarts.
  • This is still experimental, and problems can happen. The unit may still freeze now and then, and you may hear crackles or dropouts, especially when it's connected to a computer and recording into a DAW. Most of this comes from running close to the limit of the unit's processor. If it happens, take a heavy block out of the preset; if the unit freezes, turn it off and on.

On the HX Effects itself (HX Edit can't edit the new blocks). In every choice from step 2.

    

    
    
      
  1. Add a block and pick Distortion › Mono › NAM A2 Lite.
    2. 
      
  2. Turn Model to the slot number from step 4. You hear the capture.

      6
      
Play the HX amps

      
If you chose NAM + Amps or NAM + Amps + Effects in step 2.

    

    
    
      
  1. Add a block and open Distortion › Amps: all 111 of the HX Stomp's amps, 94 guitar and
        17 bass, with the Stomp's own knobs.
    2. 
      
  2. The preamps (the amps without their power amp section) are under EQ › Preamps.
    3. 
    

    
    
      
  • The HX Stomp's 41 legacy cabs are under EQ › Cabs: up to two in a preset, so one in
        each path. For a newer cab, put an IR block after the amp, with a cab IR in a User IR slot.
    • 
      
  • Up to three amps and preamps in a preset, together.
    • 
      
  • Amps are heavy blocks: next to NAM and the heavier effects, the unit may refuse one more.
    •

Play the new effects

      
If you chose NAM + Amps + Effects in step 2. Each one is in the list of the stock block it replaces (the
        stereo version of that block stays).

    

    

      
EffectWhere, and what it is

      
        
Drum ChapelDelay › Mono. A basic drum machine that follows tap
          tempo: pick a groove and a kit (seven, one with sampled acoustic drums). The footswitch starts and stops it.

        
Bolt, Rocket!Distortion › Mono. Bit crusher with 16 shaping curves.

        
Garrrry TankDistortion › Mono. Square-wave octave fuzz.

        
Quality PolisDistortion › Legacy. A square-wave synth that follows
          what you play.

        
Bam CrusherDistortion › Mono. Sequenced bit crusher with stutters.

        
Doom ChugDistortion › Mono. A low drone generator; try it into Bolt,
          Rocket!, Quality Polis, Garrrry Tank or Bam Crusher.

        
Pure WhiteyDistortion › Mono. A glitchy sampler with eight modes.

        
Greasy StranglerPitch/Synth › Mono. A drone that follows your notes and
          holds the last one.

        
BethblahemPitch/Synth › Mono. A low theremin-style synth.

        
Puggy PuncherDelay › Mono. A slot-machine glitch on every step,
          in time with tap tempo.

      
    

    
    
      
  • Several of these use a lot of the processor. Add them one at a time; the unit refuses a block when a preset is
        full. Save often.

      ?
      
Questions

    

    
Why do I need Line 6's file?
      
The firmware belongs to Line 6, so this project can't hand it out. The page takes your copy and adds the new
        parts to it on your computer.

    
Is anything uploaded?
      
No. Everything happens inside this browser tab. Your firmware and captures never leave your computer.

    
How do I know the new file is right?
      
The page checks your Line 6 file's fingerprint (MD5) first, and the finished file's fingerprint at the end.
        It only says Verified when the result is exactly, byte for byte, the file the project tested on a real
        HX Effects.

    
It says it isn't the right firmware file.
      
  • It must be HX Effects version 3.80.0, exactly as Line 6 made it. Older or newer versions don't work.
    • 
        
  • Don't pick a file made here (it ends in _NAM_Amps_Effects.hxf).
    • 
        
  • A renamed file is fine: only what's inside counts.

    
My capture says "No 3-channel A2 Lite model inside".
      
NAM captures come in several sizes, and the HX Effects can only run the A2 Lite size. Look for the A2 Lite
        version of the capture.

Rescue: put Line 6's firmware back

      
If the HX Effects won't start, stops on the splash screen or says "Boot Failure", or you just want the normal
        firmware back. Your presets, IRs and settings stay.

    

    
Don't worry: the new firmware never touches the part of the unit that starts it up, so it can always be
      rescued.


    
1. Start the HX Effects in update mode

    
    
      
  1. Quit HX Edit and Line 6 Updater. Connect the HX Effects to the computer with USB.
    2. 
      
  2. Turn it off. Hold the right arrow (>) button while you turn it on, and keep holding for about three
        seconds. It starts in update mode (the screen stays dark or shows an update message).
    3. 
    

    
If it still starts up normally, that's fine too: the
      rescue works from there as well.


    
2. Reinstall Line 6's firmware from this page

    
In Chrome or Edge: drop Line 6's own file here
      (HX_Effects_v3.80.0_0xd97239907.hxf, the one from step 1; you can download it again from
      line6.com/software), then click
      Rescue my HX Effects and pick it in the browser's list.

    
    

      
      
      Drop Line 6's file above first.

Or: with Line 6 Updater

    
If this page can't find the unit (for example on Windows, where Line 6's driver may hide it from the browser):
      start it in update mode as above, open Line 6 Updater, click the HX Effects, click Choose File and
      pick Line 6's own file. Let it finish, then turn the unit off and on.

    
Still stuck after both? Turn the unit off for a minute, try another USB cable and port, and run the
      rescue again. A failed or interrupted rescue can always be run again.
