HX Effects · NAM, Amps and Effects

Put NAM captures and amps on your HX Effects

This page does everything for you, step by step: it makes the new firmware from Line 6's own file, shows you how to install it, and turns your .nam captures into files the HX Effects can play. It works in your browser on a Mac or a Windows PC. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Unofficial and experimental. This isn't made or supported by Line 6. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk and may void your warranty. If anything goes wrong, Recovery puts the normal firmware back; your presets and IRs survive it.
Good to know before you start
  • HX Edit only works for loading files. You can still use it to import IRs and NAM captures (the .wav files from step 4) and to back up. It can't show or edit NAM, the amps or the new effects: build and edit presets that use them on the HX Effects itself.
  • Too many heavy blocks in one preset can freeze or restart the unit. NAM, the amps and some of the effects use a lot of the processor. Add heavy blocks one at a time, and save your presets often so nothing is lost if the unit restarts.

Install it

      
Two ways: straight from this page, or with Line 6 Updater. Both write the same file.

    


    

      
Option A: install from this page

      
Needs Chrome or Edge (Safari and Firefox can't reach USB devices). Works on a Mac; on
        Windows, Line 6's driver may keep the browser from seeing the unit, so use option B there if it isn't found.

      
    
        
  1. Make your firmware in step 2 first.
    2. 
        
  2. Quit HX Edit and Line 6 Updater: they hold the USB connection.
    3. 
        
  3. Connect the HX Effects with USB, turn it on, and click Install on my HX Effects. Pick the
          HX Effects in the browser's list. The unit restarts twice during the install; when the page asks,
          click Continue and pick it again (it may show up as "LPC" or an unknown device).
    4. 
        
  4. Wait about two minutes, until the page says it's done. Don't unplug or switch off the HX Effects
          meanwhile.
    5. 
      

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        

        

        
Technical details

      

    


    
Option B: install with Line 6 Updater

    
    
      
  1. Click Download your firmware in step 2.
    2. 
      
  2. Quit HX Edit if it's open. Updater can't reach the HX Effects while HX Edit is using it.
    3. 
      
  3. Connect the HX Effects to the computer with USB and turn it on.
    4. 
      
  4. Open Line 6 Updater, sign in, and click your HX Effects.
    5. 
      
  5. Click Choose File (not the normal update) and pick the downloaded file,
        HX_Effects_v3.80.0_NAM_Amps_Effects.hxf.
    6. 
      
  6. Let it finish, about two minutes. Don't unplug or switch off the HX Effects while it updates. It
        restarts by itself. If Updater says something went wrong, run it again with the same file.
    7. 
    

    
Check it worked

    
Add a block on the HX Effects and open Distortion › Mono: you'll see NAM A2
      Lite. With the amps, there's also a new Distortion › Amps group.

    
Stuck on the splash screen, or it says "Boot Failure"? Don't worry: follow Recovery.

Add your NAM captures

      
The HX Effects plays NAM captures from its User IR slots. First turn each .nam into a .wav, then
        load it into a slot.

    

    
    


    

      
No captures yet

      

        
        
      

    

    

      
Converted captures appear here. Only the A2 Lite size works (on ToneHunt and
        similar sites, look for "A2 Lite" or a file that offers several sizes).

    


    
Load them into the HX Effects

    
    
      
  1. Open HX Edit with the HX Effects connected. First pick a preset without a NAM block in it.
    2. 
      
  2. Open the Impulses list and drag a .wav onto a slot from 1 to 21. Note its number,
        for example 5.
    3. 
    

    
The .wav isn't a sound you can hear: it carries the capture's numbers. Don't use it in a Cab or IR
      block; it's only for the NAM A2 Lite block.

Play

      
On the HX Effects itself (HX Edit can't edit the new blocks).

    

    
    
      
  1. Add a block and pick Distortion › Mono › NAM A2 Lite.
    2. 
      
  2. Turn Model to the slot number from step 4. You hear the capture.
    3.

Recovery, or going back to normal

      
If the HX Effects won't start properly, or you just want Line 6's firmware back.

    

    
    
      
  1. Quit HX Edit. Turn the HX Effects off, with USB still connected.
    2. 
      
  2. Hold the right arrow (>) button while you turn it on, and keep holding for about three seconds. It
        starts in update mode.
    3. 
      
  3. In Line 6 Updater, click the HX Effects, click Choose File and pick Line 6's own file from step 1
        (HX_Effects_v3.80.0_0xd97239907.hxf). Let it finish, then turn the unit off and on.
    4. 
    

    
This works because the new firmware never touches the part of the unit that starts it up. Your presets, IRs and
      settings stay.

Questions

    

    
Why do I need Line 6's file?
      
The firmware belongs to Line 6, so this project can't hand it out. The page takes your copy and adds the new
        parts to it on your computer.

    
Is anything uploaded?
      
No. Everything happens inside this browser tab. Your firmware and captures never leave your computer.

    
How do I know the new file is right?
      
The page checks your Line 6 file's fingerprint (MD5) first, and the finished file's fingerprint at the end.
        It only says Verified when the result is exactly, byte for byte, the file the project tested on a real
        HX Effects.

    
It says it isn't the right firmware file.
      
  • It must be HX Effects version 3.80.0, exactly as Line 6 made it. Older or newer versions don't work.
    • 
        
  • Don't pick a file made here (it ends in _NAM_Amps_Effects.hxf).
    • 
        
  • A renamed file is fine: only what's inside counts.

    
My capture says "No 3-channel A2 Lite model inside".
      
NAM captures come in several sizes, and the HX Effects can only run the A2 Lite size. Look for the A2 Lite
        version of the capture.
